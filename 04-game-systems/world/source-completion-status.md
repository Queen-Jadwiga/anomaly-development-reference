## Stopping rule and runtime

Complete the selected available-source chains and audit/synthesize them before declaring the world available-source pass complete. Keep unavailable effective edges explicit. Defer independent runtime lookup/relocation/path/accessibility/use/touch/transition/marker/save-load checks until resumed under an identified executable/profile and logs.

Exhaustive graph search/AI/physics, every use target/patrol/level/marker type, renderer internals, vehicles and unrelated multiplayer are optional later depth unless a concrete question depends on them. No CtZ gameplay changes or third-party source/assets are part of this increment. [Roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).
