# C-02 — CUIScrollView internal pad and child ownership

> ADR document set: **v0.1.60**. Research revisions and source/build versions below describe their original scope.

**Baseline:** user-supplied `xray-monolith-2026.10.11.zip`. **Status:** source research complete; runtime deferred. **Scope:** native `CUIScrollView` child handling and direct Lua exports. Does not establish general Luabind userdata lifetime or safe operations on destroyed windows.

## Verified from the October 11 source

1. **Internal container initialization:** `CUIScrollView::InitScrollView()` allocates `m_pad` if absent, sets `AutoDelete(true)` and attaches it to the scroll view. It similarly allocates and attaches a scrolling bar if absent, also with auto-delete. The constructors initialize `m_pad = NULL`; the default constructor also initializes `m_VScrollBar = NULL`. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L10-L20](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L10-L20); [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L59-L92](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L59-L92).
2. **AddWindow:** `AddWindow(CUIWindow* pWnd, bool auto_delete)` sets `pWnd->SetAutoDelete(true)` **only if `auto_delete` is true**, attaches the entry to `m_pad` (not to the scroll-view's direct child list), and requests size recalculation. A `false` argument does not explicitly reset an already-true flag. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L99-L105](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L99-L105).
3. **RemoveWindow:** calls `m_pad->DetachChild(pWnd)` and requests layout recalculation. `CUIWindow::DetachChild` clears the parent and **deletes** the entry if its `IsAutoDelete()` flag is true; otherwise it detaches without native deletion. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L107-L111](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L107-L111); [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow.cpp#L300-L317](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow.cpp#L300-L317).
4. **Clear and destruction:** `Clear()` calls `m_pad->DetachAll()`, triggers layout recalculation, and scrolls to beginning. `~CUIScrollView()` explicitly invokes `Clear()`; the base `CUIWindow` destructor also detaches direct children, which include the initialized auto-delete pad and scrollbar. The pad's child removal follows normal auto-delete rules. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L42-L45](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L42-L45); [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L113-L118](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L113-L118); [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow.cpp#L183-L198](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow.cpp#L183-L198).
5. **Layout:** `RecalcSize()` computes placement and height from `m_pad` children and updates the pad size; scroll entries are not simply rendered at their original attached coordinates. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L136-L189](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIScrollView.cpp#L136-L189).
6. **Lua exports:** `UIWindow_script.cpp` exports `CUIScrollView` constructor and `AddWindow`, `RemoveWindow`, `Clear`, `ScrollToBegin/End`, scroll position getters, `SetFixedScrollBar`, and `SetScrollPos`. `AddWindow` is bound **without an explicit `luabind::adopt<2>()` policy** at this registration site, unlike `CUIWindow::AttachChild`. This is an observable binding distinction, **not** proof of any particular Lua-GC lifetime behavior. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow_script.cpp#L329-L340](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow_script.cpp#L329-L340); [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow_script.cpp#L202-L209](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ui/UIWindow_script.cpp#L202-L209).
7. **XML attachment route:** `CScriptXmlInit::_attach_child` recognizes scroll parents and calls `AddWindow(_child, true)`, so controls created through that factory with a scroll parent become auto-delete pad entries. [https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ScriptXMLInit.cpp#L28-L38](https://github.com/themrdemonized/xray-monolith/blob/2026.10.11/src/xrGame/ScriptXMLInit.cpp#L28-L38).

## Limits and careful implications

- `AddWindow`, `RemoveWindow`, and `Clear` dereference `m_pad`; the inspected methods do not check whether `InitScrollView()` has run. Therefore, initializing before adding/removing is a **source-supported precondition** for ordinary use; this does not establish every XML construction pathway.
- Avoid reusing native pointers or Lua references to entries after a `RemoveWindow` / `Clear` that deletes them. The source does not prove whether a Lua userdata is invalidated or protected when native deletion occurs.
- The `auto_delete` flag controls **native** deletion; no claim is made about whether Luabind continues owning the entry after `AddWindow` on the Lua side. The lack of explicit adoption makes this a separate concern for a later focused investigation.
- Do **not** conclude that calling `Clear()` on an uninitialized scroll view is safe, because it dereferences `m_pad`. Destructor invokes it, but ordinary lifecycle assumptions need separate investigation.

## CtZ-specific application

For War PDA lists and the War Ledger: initialize scroll views through a proven XML/native path before adding rows; choose one documented ownership model for dynamically created rows; do not preserve stale Lua pointers after a native auto-delete. Keep virtualized list patterns and Lua ownership semantics for later source audits, and verify repeated open/close, clearing, updating, and save/load in a later runtime batch.
