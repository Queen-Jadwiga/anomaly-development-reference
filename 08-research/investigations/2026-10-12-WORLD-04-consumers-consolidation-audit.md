## Competing explanations and remaining edges

Equal selected script blocks do not establish a winning full file/profile. Missing map/zone/helper files do not imply missing game features. Marker cache differences, preset typos and commented alternate serializers are source facts, not reproduced bugs. Loading aliases, callback priority, options/includes, durable tables and native/live readiness still require effective evidence. No third-party script is patched or redistributed.
